The Physics of FLRW Spacetimes

The Cosmological Principle

The FLRW models are based on the assumption that on large scales, the universe is homogeneous (the same everywhere) and isotropic (looks the same in all directions). This principle dramatically simplifies Einstein's equations, leading to the Friedmann equations.

The Friedmann Equations

These equations describe a cosmic tug-of-war. The expansion of the universe (the "Hubble flow") is constantly fighting against the inward pull of gravity from the matter and energy within it. The density parameter Ω_m determines the winner of this battle.

Spacelike vs. Timelike Singularities

In a closed universe (Ω_m > 1), the Big Bang and Big Crunch are spacelike singularities. They are moments in time that everyone in the universe experiences. In contrast, the singularity inside a black hole is timelike—it's a place in space that one could theoretically avoid.
