The Universal Laws & Maximal Depth

String Theory Landscape

Instead of a single potential, string theory predicts a vast "landscape" of possible vacuums, each with its own cosmological constant. Our universe may have started in a high-energy vacuum and then "tunneled" to our current low-energy state. This is a leading explanation for the smallness of the observed dark energy.

Extra Spacetime Dimensions

String theory also requires extra spatial dimensions. These could be curled up into tiny, compact spaces, invisible to our current experiments. The geometry of these extra dimensions would affect the laws of physics we observe in our 4D spacetime.

The Measure Problem

In an eternally-inflating multiverse, any event that can happen will happen an infinite number of times. This makes it difficult to calculate probabilities. For example, how do we determine whether we are "typical" observers? The "measure problem" is the challenge of finding a sensible way to regulate these infinities and make predictions. Different proposed measures can lead to vastly different conclusions about the nature of our universe.
