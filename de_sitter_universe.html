The Physics of an Accelerating Cosmos

The de Sitter Solution

De Sitter space is the maximally symmetric vacuum solution to Einstein's equations with a positive cosmological constant (Λ). It describes an empty universe undergoing exponential expansion. This is a key model for both the inflationary epoch and the potential far future of our own universe.

Observer-Dependent Horizons

Unlike a black hole, which has a single, absolute event horizon, every observer in de Sitter space has their own private cosmological event horizon. This isn't a physical barrier, but a point of no return created by the accelerating expansion of space itself. If another galaxy crosses your horizon, it has been carried away from you faster than the speed of light.

Anatomy of the de Sitter Square

The Penrose diagram for de Sitter space is a square. The top and bottom edges represent future and past timelike infinity (i⁺ and i⁻), where comoving observers begin and end their journeys. The left and right corners are future and past null infinity (ℐ⁺ and ℐ⁻), where light rays enter and leave the spacetime.
